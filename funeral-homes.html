Common Questions

What funeral homes
ask us first.

What does it cost to try it?
Nothing. The first song is free — no cost to you and no cost to the family. You see what it does in the room before you decide whether to offer it again.
How much time does this require from our staff?
Minimal. Your team asks one question during arrangements and hands the family a printed one-pager showing what questions to expect and the link to The Story Room, where they submit their story. After that, the family handles everything on their own time — Tim handles production, delivery, and follow-up. Your staff isn't involved beyond that handoff.
What if the family doesn't want to fill out a form?
Then they don't. Most families use the online intake because they can do it at their own pace, together, at home. But a family that would rather talk it through can call Tim and do it as a conversation. Either way, your staff isn't involved.
What if a family doesn't want it?
No problem. There's no pressure and no obligation. You only pay when a family says yes and Tim delivers the finished song. Families who decline don't cost you anything.
Can we brand the song under our funeral home's name?
Yes. The song is presented to the family as a service your funeral home arranged and provided. Tim works in the background — the relationship stays yours.
How is the song actually delivered?
Your call. Within 24 hours of receiving the family's story, the song is finished and lives on a permanent private page of its own. You can present that link to the family yourself, in person, as part of your final meeting with them — or Tim can send it directly to the family and you never touch it. Most homes prefer the second: it's one less thing to manage, and the song is still presented as something your funeral home arranged for them. Either way, the relationship and the credit stay yours.
Is there any commitment to get started?
None. No contract, no minimum order count, no setup fee. Reach out to Tim to get your question card materials and you're ready to offer it on your next arrangement.
