Common Questions

What funeral homes
ask us first.

How much time does this require from our staff?
Minimal. Your team asks one question during arrangements and hands the family a printed one-pager showing what questions to expect and a link to submit their story. After that, the family handles everything on their own time — Tim handles production, delivery, and follow-up. Your staff isn't involved beyond that handoff.
What if a family doesn't want it?
No problem. There's no pressure and no obligation. You only pay when a family says yes and Tim delivers the finished song. Families who decline don't cost you anything.
Can we brand the song under our funeral home's name?
Yes. The song is presented to the family as a service your funeral home arranged and provided. Tim works in the background — the relationship stays yours.
How is the song actually delivered?
Tim delivers the completed song — MP3, MP4, album art, lyric sheet, and a private YouTube link — directly to you within 24 hours of the family submitting their story. You then pass it along to the family however fits your workflow.
Is there any commitment to get started?
None. No contract, no minimum order count, no setup fee. Reach out to Tim to get your question card materials and you're ready to offer it on your next arrangement.
